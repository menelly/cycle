🧩 🧠 CORE MODULES — THE FOUNDATIONAL ONES
These should cover most "normal planner" needs with zero chaos:

Monthly Grid – classic calendar view

Weekly Overview – Mon–Sun boxes (horizontal or vertical)

Daily Layout – broken into Morning / Afternoon / Evening

Hourly Schedule – 6am to 10pm or customizable

Task List / To-Do List – with checkbox rows

Notes Section – blank or lined box

Habit Tracker – grid format, days vs habits

Goals Section – with long/short term split

Important Dates – like a mini timeline

Calendar Strip – 1-week horizontal scroll bar with mini dates

🪄 ADHD/NEURODIVERGENT-FRIENDLY MODULES
Brain Dump Box – big messy text field, no structure, just vibe-purge

What's the Vibe? – mood tracker with custom emojis or sliders

Executive Dysfunction Timer – write a thing, then start a timer for doing the thing

Novelty Tracker – box that changes color/text/image on schedule

Energy Check-In – AM/PM energy levels (1-5 spoons or lightning bolts ⚡)

Body/Brain Status – dropdowns or emoji selectors for pain, focus, stim level, etc

Reward System – "If I do these 3 things, I get X" tracker

Daily Wins – a place to write one tiny win a day

Guilt-Free Skip Tracker – "I skipped this and the world didn't end" checkbox 💀✅

💼 BUSINESS/SELLER-USEFUL MODULES
Order Tracker – order #, product, ship date

Income Tracker – date, source, amount

Product Launch Countdown – milestone list

Content Planner – platform, date, content, status

Marketing Checklist – reusable launch checkboxes

Client Notes – lined section with name/date

Monthly Earnings Summary – chart box

Template Usage Tracker – if they reuse layouts to sell new planners

🫀 WELLNESS + LIFE MODULES
Meal Planner – week grid or freeform

Water Tracker – 8 cups/drops/squares

Symptom Log – tags, checkboxes, free text

Medication Reminder – time, dose, checkbox

Period Tracker – calendar-style or dot grid

Gratitude Box – list 3 things per day

Sleep Tracker – hours slept / quality slider

👻 FUN CHAOS + SELF-EXPRESSION MODULES
Cat of the Day – image box that rotates ��

Quote Generator – "motivational" or gremlin style

Sticker Wall – reward system where checked items spawn stickers

What Day Is It Again? – box that yells TODAY IS: [day/date/time/planet alignment]

Gremlin Affirmation Generator – things like "You survived capitalism again. 10 XP."

Moodboard Module – image uploads or links

🔧 STRUCTURAL / UTILITY MODULES
Divider / Section Header

Mini Table / Grid

Two-Column Layout Block

Freeform Layout Box (drag anything into it)

Template Loader – drop in full daily/weekly/monthly templates from saved ones

QR Code Block – link to something (e.g. "scan for grocery list")
